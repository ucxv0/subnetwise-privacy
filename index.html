Subnetwise — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-05-19. Applies to Subnetwise version 1.0 and later.

Short version: Subnetwise collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing outside your device. It has no internet permission, so it cannot phone home even if it wanted to.

What Subnetwise is

Subnetwise is an Android subnet calculator. It runs entirely on your device. All calculations happen locally. Nothing is transmitted off the device because the app cannot transmit anything off the device.

Data we collect

None.

Subnetwise does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store any personal data, usage data, diagnostic data, or device identifiers. There is no account to create, no login, no profile, no telemetry.

Permissions

Subnetwise requests zero runtime permissions. The AndroidManifest.xml does not declare android.permission.INTERNET, so the operating system itself prevents the app from making any network connection.

You can verify this on your device: long-press the Subnetwise icon, open App info, and check the Permissions section. It will be empty.

Third parties

Subnetwise includes no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no social-login SDKs. It does not embed trackers of any kind. The only third party involved in your use of the app is Google Play itself, which handles the purchase and installation. Google Play's privacy practices are governed by Google's privacy policy, not this one.

Data stored on your device

Subnetwise stores the following locally on your device using Android's DataStore, never leaving the device:

  • Your saved (bookmarked) subnets and their labels
  • Your theme preference (System / Light / Dark)
  • Which Learn lessons you have read

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or use Android's "Clear data" option in App info.

Children's privacy

Subnetwise does not collect any data from anyone, including children. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new effective date at the top. Since the app collects no data, changes are expected to be rare and minor (clarifications only).
